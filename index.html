Model answer · 320 words

It is argued that zoos are cruel places that should be shut down, while others believe that they can play a useful role in protecting wild animals. Although both views have some merit, I believe that zoos are beneficial as long as animals are properly cared for.

On the one hand, those who oppose zoos argue that animals in captivity are often mistreated. Many zoos fail to provide sufficient food and proper medical care, which can lead to starvation and diseases. As a result, the number of individuals in some species populations may fall even further. Furthermore, critics claim that keeping zoos open is a waste of national resources. Although governments provide financial support and human resources to zoos, many animals living in cages still die, which proves that such investments are not worthwhile. Instead, this money could be used to fund conservation programmes that allow animals to live in their natural environments.

On the other hand, I believe that zoos can be effective in protecting wild animals. In the wild, many species face serious threats such as poaching and deforestation, which destroy their habitats and push them towards extinction. By running breeding programmes and conservation campaigns, zoos can help these animals avoid becoming extinct. A typical example is the black-footed ferret, which was saved from extinction largely thanks to breeding programmes in North American zoos. In addition, zoos raise public awareness of animal protection, especially among young schoolchildren. Through interactive activities, visitors are encouraged to learn more about biodiversity and wildlife preservation, which makes learning more attractive and vivid. Consequently, people learn to value the environment and animals, thereby becoming more responsible for animal protection.

In conclusion, although some zoos may harm animals and waste public money, I believe that well-managed zoos play an important role in protecting wildlife and educating the public. Therefore, rather than being closed down, zoos should be improved so that animals receive better care.
